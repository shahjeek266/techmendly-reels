Stop clicking through 20 tabs. ⌨️

Ctrl + 1 to 8: jump to that tab
Ctrl + 9: jump to the last tab
Ctrl + Tab: next tab
Ctrl + Shift + Tab: previous tab

On Mac use Cmd for number jumps. 📌 Save this.
