Getting boring, generic answers from AI? 😩 The problem is usually the prompt, not the AI.

Use this 3-part formula:
1️⃣ WHO: give it a role ("You are a social media manager for a bakery")
2️⃣ WHAT: state the exact task ("Write 3 Instagram captions for a new chocolate cake")
3️⃣ HOW: pick the format ("Short and friendly")

Specific in, useful out. 📌 Save this for your next prompt.
