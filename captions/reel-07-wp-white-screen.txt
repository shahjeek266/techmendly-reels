Blank white page on your WordPress site? 😱 Don't panic. Most often it is one plugin or theme.

1️⃣ Connect with FTP or your host's file manager
2️⃣ Rename wp-content/plugins to plugins-off
3️⃣ Reload. If the site is back, rename it back and turn plugins on one by one

Still blank? Switch to a default theme the same way. 📌 Save this.
