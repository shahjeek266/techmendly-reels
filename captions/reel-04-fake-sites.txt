Is that website real, or a fake? 🕵️ Check these 3 things before you click:

1️⃣ Read the web address. One extra word means it's fake.
2️⃣ Hover over a link before you click it and see where it really goes.
3️⃣ Urgent messages are a red flag. Go to the site yourself instead.

Not sure? Don't click. Open the site from your own bookmark.
