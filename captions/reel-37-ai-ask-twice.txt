Want to catch an AI making things up? Ask it twice. 🤖

1. Open two fresh chats.
2. Ask both the exact same question.
3. Compare the names, dates and numbers.

If the facts disagree, the AI may be guessing, so don't trust that answer. If they match, it's a good sign but not proof. Check anything important in a trusted source.
