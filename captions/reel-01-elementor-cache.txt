Saved a change in Elementor but your live page still looks old? 😩

Your change is almost certainly saved. Your site keeps several copies of every page, and one of them is stale.

Clear them in this order:
1️⃣ Elementor > Tools > Clear Files & Data
2️⃣ Cache plugin or host cache > Purge All
3️⃣ Cloudflare > Caching > Purge
4️⃣ Check in a private window

Inside out, so no layer re-caches the old page.
