Site broke after an update? Find the culprit in minutes. 🔧

1️⃣ Plugins > Installed Plugins > select all > Deactivate
2️⃣ Reload the site. Fixed? A plugin is the cause.
3️⃣ Turn plugins back on one at a time, reloading after each

The one that breaks it again is your culprit.
